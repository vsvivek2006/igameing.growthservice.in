**Cadence**: First business day of each calendar month  
**Objective**: Long-term topical authority evaluation, non-branded search expansion, and conversion correlation.

---

## 2. Category Performance Deep-Dive

### A. Industry Vertical Pillars (Group C)
- Review impressions and clicks for each of the 8 verticals (`gaming`, `casino`, `cricket-gaming`, `yono`, `color-prediction`, `color-trading`, `stock-market`, `adult-escort`).
- Identify which verticals are establishing algorithmic authority and which require supporting editorial link equity.

### B. Core Services (Group B)
- Review organic engagement across the 12 specialist service hubs.
- Verify that high-intent commercial terms (e.g. "technical seo audit", "casino web development") drive visitors directly to relevant service deliverables.

### C. Service × Industry Matrix (Group D)
- Review penetration of the 12 approved matrix pages.
- Evaluate search volume trends for specialized combination terms.

### D. Knowledge Hub & Resource Assisted Conversions (Group E & F)
- Trace path-to-conversion for users landing on guides:
  - `Guide Landing` -> `Service Page Click` -> `Audit Submission`.
- Calculate the assisted conversion rate for educational content.

---

## 3. Algorithmic Volatility & SERP Landscape Analysis

- Document major search engine core updates, helpful content system shifts, or platform policy revisions.
- Distinguish between true ranking fluctuations and external factors (seasonality, holiday gaming volume, sports event schedules).

---

## 4. 30-Day Prioritized Roadmap

Define maximum 3 major initiatives for the upcoming month:
1. **Initiative 1 (P0/P1)**: Technical refinement, structured data expansion, or core conversion form optimization.
2. **Initiative 2 (P1/P2)**: Deepening content and adding technical illustrations to 2 striking-distance pages.
3. **Initiative 3 (P2)**: Contextual cross-linking audit between newly matured guides and commercial service pages.
